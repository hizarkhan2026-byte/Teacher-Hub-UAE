Make the APK for free in your browser (no installs)

1. Create a free account at github.com
2. Click + > New repository. Name it teacher-hub. Create it.
3. Click "uploading an existing file". Unzip this folder, then drag ALL its
   files and folders in, including the ".github" folder. Click Commit changes.
4. Open the Actions tab. If asked, click "I understand my workflows, enable them".
5. Click "Build APK" > Run workflow. Wait about 5 to 10 minutes.
6. Open the finished run, scroll to Artifacts, download Teacher-Hub-APK.
7. Unzip it: app-debug.apk is your app. Copy it to your phone and install.
